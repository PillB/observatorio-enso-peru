Personal information we collect

The public website does not ask you to register, log in or submit any form, and it does not use cookies. The personal information we may process is limited to the following.

Information you provide to us. Personal information you may provide to us through the Service or otherwise includes:

  • Communications data based on our exchanges with you, including when you contact us by email.
  • Review console account data, only for members of the team who review and correct the published data: the username, a one-way hash of the password (the password itself is never stored), the session and a log of the actions taken in the console, with the IP address from which each action was made.

Third-party sources. The Service republishes information from public sources, such as government agencies, public records and news media (for example, the headline, date and a short excerpt of a news item with a link to it). This information may occasionally mention individuals.

Automatic data collection. Our hosting providers may automatically log information about your computer or mobile device and your interaction with the Service, such as:

  • Device data, such as your IP address, browser type and version, operating system and language settings.
  • Online activity data, such as the pages you requested, the time of each request and the website you visited before browsing to the Service.

For more information concerning our automatic collection of data, please see the Tracking & Other Technologies section below.

Tracking & Other Technologies

No cookies on the public website. The public website does not set cookies and does not load third-party scripts, fonts, images or advertising. The review consoles use a single session cookie, needed to keep team members logged in.

Browser storage. The website stores your preferences in your own browser (local storage): the light or dark theme, the version of each map you chose and your progress in the guided tour. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Anonymous map statistics. When you leave a page, the website sends one message per map with the version you saw and what you did with it (for example, whether you filtered or opened an item). Our server only adds one to a daily counter for that version and event; it does not store your IP address, any identifier or any cookie. If your browser sends a “Do Not Track” or Global Privacy Control signal, nothing is sent.
