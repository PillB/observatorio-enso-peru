   260→## 4.1 Evidence categories
   261→
   262→Every displayed result must be identified as one of:
   263→
   264→- direct observation;

   271→- internal operational signal;
   272→- reanalysis;
   273→- model output;
   274→- interpolation;
   275→- illustrative visualization;
   276→- cached fallback;
   277→- missing or unavailable information.
   278→
   279→Do not allow one category to impersonate another.
   280→
   281→## 4.2 No fabricated data
   282→
   283→Never:
   284→
   285→- invent missing values;
   286→- silently forward-fill;
   287→- substitute another indicator;
   288→- infer coastal state from basin state;
   289→- infer basin state from coastal state;
   290→- label an unavailable indicator as neutral;
   291→- replace an official status with a threshold classification;
   292→- call a raw wind index an anomaly without proof;
   293→- call an approximate local calculation official.
   294→
   295→## 4.3 RONI
   296→
   297→Validate directly against current NOAA/CPC methodology.
   298→
   299→Official RONI must not be described merely as:
   300→
   301→- Niño 3.4;
   302→- ONI;
   303→- a local three-month average;
   304→- an adaptive 30-day baseline;
   305→- or a generic moving climatology.
   306→
   307→Record and correctly present:
   308→
   309→- ERSSTv5;
   310→- relative Niño 3.4 anomaly;
   311→- subtraction of the tropical-mean SST anomaly;
   312→- variance adjustment;
   313→- 1991–2020 base period;
   314→- overlapping three-month seasons;
   315→- relevant threshold and persistence rules;
   316→- revision of recent values;
   317→- official publication cadence.
   318→
   319→Prefer ingestion of the official published RONI series.
   320→
   321→A locally reproduced approximation must have a separate name and must not replace the official value.
   322→
   323→## 4.4 ICEN
   324→
   325→Validate against the latest ENFEN methodology.
   326→
   327→Record:
   328→
   329→- current methodology version;
   330→- ERSSTv5;
   331→- Niño 1+2 coordinates;
   332→- 1991–2020 climatology;
   333→- three-month running mean;
   334→- current classification thresholds;
   335→- persistence requirements;
   336→- provisional and final states;
   337→- distinction between ICEN classification and official ENFEN declaration.
   338→
   339→## 4.5 SOI
   340→
   341→Verify:
   342→
   343→- Tahiti–Darwin construction;
   344→- standardization;
   345→- sign;
   346→- cadence;
   347→- observation date;
   348→- revision state.
   349→
   350→Retain the explicit statement that the conventional SOI is basin-scale and that the observatory does not invent a “coastal SOI.”
   351→
   352→## 4.6 Winds
   353→
   354→Determine separately for every wind source:
   355→
   356→- actual wind or anomaly;
   357→- zonal component or vector;
   358→- standardized index or physical units;
   359→- 850 hPa or surface;
   360→- western, central, eastern, or full Pacific;
   361→- spatial bounds;
   362→- temporal aggregation;
   363→- climatology;
   364→- sign convention;
   365→- product continuity.
   366→
   367→Names, units, tooltips, charts, and assistant answers must match the actual product.
   368→
   369→## 4.7 D20
   370→
   371→Verify:
   372→
   373→- actual source variable;
   374→- spatial aggregation;
   375→- model or assimilation system;
   376→- climatology;
   377→- sign;
   378→- units;
   379→- observation period;
   380→- missing-data behavior;
   381→- revision policy.
   382→
   383→Do not claim a coastal D20 state from a basin-scale average.
   384→
   385→## 4.8 Forecasts
   386→
   387→Every forecast must identify:
   388→
   389→- issuing institution;
   390→- issue date;
   391→- valid season;
   392→- probability;
   393→- verifying index;
   394→- expert or model basis;
   395→- uncertainty;
   396→- limitations.
   397→
   398→Do not transform current observations into an unofficial forecast.
